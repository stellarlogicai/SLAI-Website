# ServicesOS Known Limitations During Beta

Use this document to set expectations before wife beta and early internal testing. These limitations are acceptable for beta as long as they are clear, documented, and do not block the core workflow.

## UI May Still Need Polish

- Limitation: Some screens, spacing, labels, or visual states may need refinement.
- Why it is acceptable for beta: Beta is focused on whether the workflow works and makes sense.
- Useful feedback: Note confusing labels, hard-to-find buttons, mobile spacing problems, or places where the next step is unclear.

## Payments May Still Be in Testing / Stabilization

- Limitation: Payment behavior may still be in testing, stabilization, or status-tracking mode.
- Why it is acceptable for beta: The first priority is validating the lead-to-job workflow before relying on payment collection.
- Useful feedback: Note whether payment status is understandable, whether the owner can tell what is paid or pending, and whether wording feels misleading.

## Tap to Pay Is Not Part of This Beta

- Limitation: Tap to Pay is later and should not be treated as beta-critical.
- Why it is acceptable for beta: Tap to Pay should wait until the core workflow and payment flow are stable.
- Useful feedback: Capture Tap to Pay ideas as later items only. Do not let them block beta testing.

## Some Automation May Be Manual for Now

- Limitation: Some repeat steps may still require manual action.
- Why it is acceptable for beta: Manual workflow is acceptable while testing whether the steps, data, and statuses are correct.
- Useful feedback: Note where manual work feels confusing, repetitive, or likely to cause mistakes.

## Employee App / Workflow May Need Refinement

- Limitation: Cleaner-facing steps, checklist flow, notes, photos, and completion may need refinement.
- Why it is acceptable for beta: Wife beta should reveal what a cleaner needs before broader customer testing.
- Useful feedback: Note what a cleaner would miss, what would annoy them, and what would make completing a job easier.

## Training / SOP Content May Be Incomplete

- Limitation: Help links, SOPs, or training content may not be complete.
- Why it is acceptable for beta: The beta can still test whether the app has a clear place for help content.
- Useful feedback: Note where a cleaner or owner would get stuck and what help content would be needed.

## Advanced Analytics May Be Limited

- Limitation: Analytics, reporting, and advanced dashboards may be limited.
- Why it is acceptable for beta: Core workflow stability comes before advanced reporting.
- Useful feedback: Note which basic counts or status summaries are needed to understand daily operations.

## Future Vertical Modules Are Not Active Yet

- Limitation: Food truck, pharmacy, retail, GrowthAI, EducationOS, ComplianceAI, FutureAI, and other future vertical ideas are not active in this beta.
- Why it is acceptable for beta: ServicesOS for cleaning and service businesses is priority one.
- Useful feedback: Keep future vertical ideas separate from the ServicesOS beta-critical list.
