# ServicesOS Beta Release Notes

## Purpose of This Beta

ServicesOS is the active priority for SLAI. This internal beta is meant to validate the core workflow for cleaning and service businesses, not prove every future feature.

Wife beta comes before wider customer testing. The goal is to find blockers, confusing steps, missing workflow pieces, and places where the app does not match how a real cleaning business works.

This beta should answer:

- Can a basic cleaning job move from lead to completion?
- Can the owner/admin understand what is happening?
- Can the cleaner understand the assigned work?
- Does important customer and job information stay connected?
- Are there any blockers that must be fixed before showing anyone else?

## What Should Be Tested

- Login.
- Customer and lead creation.
- Lead status changes.
- Estimate workflow.
- Contract or booking flow if available.
- Scheduling.
- Employee assignment.
- Employee checklist and job completion.
- Admin status visibility.
- Basic payment status tracking if available.
- Dashboard counts and status updates.
- Confusing wording, missing information, and unclear steps.

## What Should Not Be Judged Yet

- Perfect UI polish.
- Final public launch readiness.
- Final pricing.
- Full automation.
- Tap to Pay.
- Future verticals or modules.
- GrowthAI, EducationOS, ComplianceAI, or FutureAI.
- Advanced analytics.

Tap to Pay is later. It should not block this beta and should not be treated as beta-critical until the core workflow and normal payment flow are stable.

Future verticals are not part of this beta. The focus is ServicesOS for cleaning and service business workflow testing.

## What Counts as a Serious Issue

A serious issue is anything that blocks or misleads the core ServicesOS workflow.

Examples:

- Cannot log in.
- Tenant or account data does not load.
- Customer or lead cannot be created.
- Estimate, booking, schedule, assignment, or job completion flow breaks.
- Employee cannot see or complete assigned work.
- Admin cannot see job progress.
- Data disappears after refresh.
- Payment status or job status is misleading.
- The app is too confusing to complete a basic job.

## What Feedback Is Most Useful

Useful beta feedback is specific and tied to a real step.

Capture:

- What you were trying to do.
- What happened.
- What you expected.
- What confused you.
- Whether it blocked the test.
- What information was missing.
- What would make the step easier for an owner or cleaner.

Less useful feedback:

- Broad feature ideas not tied to the current workflow.
- Future module ideas.
- Cosmetic preferences that do not affect testing.
- Requests for Tap to Pay before core workflow and payments are stable.

## What Happens After Beta Feedback

After each beta session:

1. Record bugs and confusion points.
2. Mark blocker and beta-critical issues.
3. Add issues to the fix queue.
4. Decide what must be fixed before the next beta run.
5. Retest fixed issues with the same scenario.
6. Continue wife beta only when blocker and beta-critical issues are cleared or have a documented workaround.
